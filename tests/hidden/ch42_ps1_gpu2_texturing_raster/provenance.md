# provenance — tests/hidden/ch42_ps1_gpu2_texturing_raster

`roms/unseen.bin` is the hidden coding-test stream (CODING_TEST.md): an
8bpp texture on an odd texture page at Y=256, wrapped through a 32-texel
GP0(E2h) texture window, rendered as two modulated `24h` triangles forming
a quadrilateral plus one variable-size `64h` textured rectangle; 256-entry
CLUT uploaded to (256,300). Dither disabled, no semi-transparency — same
documented baseline rules as the rest of the PS1 block.

Assembled by the same throwaway word-level generator as the public fixtures
(`roms/unseen.asm.txt` lists every word).

Golden: FNV-1a-64 over the runner's `--hash-frame` payload (full VRAM dump),
generated by the reference solution (`99_coding_test` runner, solution mode)
twice with byte-compare in between:

```
ch42_99_coding_test_runner --rom roms/unseen.bin --hash-frame /tmp/out.hash
# fnv64=7941D811C96BC62D
```

The manifest pins that value; students must reproduce it bit-exactly by
implementing the three marked stages in `coding_stages.hpp`.
