beep_counter — sets ST=7 (beep), then leaves a trail of dots at (DT,0) as the
delay timer counts 8->1, and marks (0,3) when DT hits zero. Beep ends after
7 ticks, before the trail finishes. Course-original.
200: 67 08   LD V7,8
202: 61 00   LD V1,0
204: A2 28   LD I,$228
206: 68 07   LD V8,7
208: F8 18   LD ST,V8
20A: 63 09   LD V3,9      ; prev sentinel
20C: F7 15   LD DT,V7
20E: F0 07   LD V0,DT     ; wait loop
210: 90 30   SNE V0,V3    ; skip next when DT changed
212: 12 0E   JP $20E
214: D0 11   DRW V0,V1,1  ; new value -> draw trail dot
216: 83 00   LD V3,V0     ; prev = current
218: F0 07   LD V0,DT
21A: 30 00   SE V0,0
21C: 12 0E   JP $20E
21E: 60 00   LD V0,0
220: 61 03   LD V1,3
222: D0 11   DRW V0,V1,1  ; end marker at (0,3)
224: 12 24   JP $224      ; halt
226: 00 00   padding
228: 80      dot sprite
