output identical both times.

## Fixtures

Produced by `tools/gen_scene.py` (committed next to this file):

    python3 tools/gen_scene.py fixtures

- `fixtures/scene_grid.nesf`   — checkerboard, four bg palettes, vertical mirroring
- `fixtures/scene_attr.nesf`   — attribute quadrant wall, horizontal mirroring
- `fixtures/scene_mirror.nesf` — art in physical page 1 only; visible through
  horizontal mirroring + PPUCTRL nametable select 2 ($2800)

NESF v1 layout: see `templates/ch21_nes_ppu1/03_render_frame/fixture.hpp`.

    ch21_03_render_runner --rom fixtures/<scene>.nesf --frames 1 \
